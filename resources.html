Helpful resources

Affiliate · Language test

IELTS / CELPIP preparation

Citizenship requires proof of English or French proficiency at CLB 4 or higher. Official practice tests and study courses help most applicants pass on the first try.

Sponsored · Legal help

Find an immigration lawyer or RCIC

A licensed Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant or immigration lawyer can review your application before submission, especially helpful if your immigration history is complex.

Affiliate · Documents

Certified document translation

Foreign documents must be translated by a certified translator and accompanied by an affidavit. The translation has to be accepted by IRCC.

Affiliate · Finance

Newcomer bank account

Several Canadian banks offer no-fee chequing for new Canadians, with interest, no monthly fees, and a free first credit card to begin building Canadian credit history.
