Calculator results are estimates

The calculator on this site applies the standard physical presence rules to the information you enter. The output is an estimate. It can be wrong if:

  • You entered an incorrect arrival date, PR date, or trip date
  • Your situation involves rules the calculator does not implement (Crown servant, prison time, removal orders, complex protected person history, etc.)
  • IRCC interprets a specific period of your status differently than the calculator does
  • Rules change after the calculator was last updated

Do not rely on the calculator output as the final word on your eligibility. Before submitting any application to IRCC, run your numbers through the official IRCC physical presence calculator at canada.ca, and consider a paid consultation with a licensed professional.
